description: Interior scenes, dining, and social gatherings — food, dress, furnishings, and event-type subject terms
applies_to: photo, verso
---

- Name specific foods, dishes, tableware, and serving vessels where they are visible, rather than writing 'food' or 'dishes'.
- Describe clothing styles, furniture, and décor by name — the period and social register of a room are usually readable from these details.
- Describe the interior in detail: room type, lighting, wall and floor treatments, signage, and visible architectural features.
- Prefer event-type subject terms where the occasion is legible: 'Banquets', 'Wedding receptions', 'Street vendors', 'Family gatherings', 'Restaurant kitchens'.
