---
name: correspondence
description: Letters, memos, and cards — sender/recipient, dateline, and letterhead conventions
applies_to: document
---

- Identify the sender and recipient from the salutation, signature, letterhead, and any envelope; put both in people.
- Use the dateline as the item date when present. If the year is abbreviated or missing, record what is written and note the ambiguity in uncertainty_notes.
- Record the return address, letterhead address, and postmark in places, keeping them distinct from places merely mentioned in the body text.
- Note the physical form in the description: handwritten or typed, letterhead or plain, single sheet or bifolium, enclosures, and any later annotations in a different hand.
- Do not paraphrase the letter in place of describing it — the description says what the document is and what it concerns; the transcription carries the wording.
